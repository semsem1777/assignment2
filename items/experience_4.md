## Bible Recitation Competition

**What I did:** I took part in a Bible recitation competition, memorising and reciting different scriptures.

**When:** 4th grade.

**What was good:** The happiness that came with successfully memorising each scripture.

**Favourite memory:** Making it to the last 3.

**What I didn't enjoy:** My dad being hard on me about it, but it paid off in the end.

**Grade:** B. For the most part enjoyable.
