## Playing in Football Academies

**What I did:** I played football for a couple of academies and teams, training regularly and competing in matches and tournaments.

**When:** 2014 to 2018

**What was good:** I really enjoyed applying what I had learnt when the time came. Drills and coaching felt worthwhile when I could use them in a real game.

**Favourite memory:** Scoring a hat-trick during an interschool competition.

**What I didn't enjoy:** The endurance drills. They were tough and I never looked forward to them.

**Grade:** B. For the most part enjoyable.
